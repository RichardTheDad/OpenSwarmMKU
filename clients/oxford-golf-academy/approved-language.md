## Tone Words

Use these to guide the voice in every piece of content:
- **Local** — This is a neighborhood business, not a chain
- **Friendly** — Approachable even to someone who's never held a club
- **Professional** — Knowledgeable, credible, not sloppy
- **Clear** — Plain language, not golf jargon the beginner won't understand
- **Helpful** — Every post should give the reader something useful or a reason to act
- **Not hype-heavy** — Avoid superlatives, "amazing," "incredible," "game-changing"
- **Not corporate** — No buzzwords, no stiff language
- **Not generic** — Every post should be specific to Oxford Golf Academy, not copyable to any golf facility
