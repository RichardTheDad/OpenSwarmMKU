## Tone Words and Patterns

Every piece of content should feel:
- **Local** — Oxford and The Villages, not generic "Central Florida"
- **Warm** — approachable, no intimidation for beginners
- **Credible** — back claims with specifics (names, numbers, confirmed services)
- **Practical** — tell readers what they'll get, what it costs or how to find out, how to take the next step
- **Direct** — verb-led sentences, short CTAs, no passive filler

**Sentence patterns that work:**
- "[Benefit]. [How OGA delivers it.] [CTA.]"
- "Working on your [area]? Our [program/clinic] covers [specific thing]. $X per session."
- "[Instructor name], [credential], has [specific fact]. [Relevant to the reader how?]"
