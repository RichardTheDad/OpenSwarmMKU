## Service 2: Training Programs (4 Programs)

Structured multi-session programs focused on one area of improvement. Four programs available:

### The Basics
**Description:** For recreational players who want improvement without a complete rebuild. Focuses on foundational mechanics — grip, setup, posture, alignment, and swing fundamentals.
**Who it's for:** Golfers who want more confidence and cleaner contact without overhauling their game
**Includes:** Grip, setup, posture, and alignment review; key swing fundamentals; dependable baseline for practice

### Hitting It Farther
**Description:** Targets increased club-head speed and ball speed through comprehensive analysis of speed, mobility, and equipment efficiency.
**Who it's for:** Golfers who want more distance
**Includes:** Speed and mobility review; equipment and technical efficiency conversation; clear practice priorities for distance gains

### Putting Master
**Description:** For players who know lower scores start on the green. Combines stroke technique with green reading and confidence-building.
**Who it's for:** Any golfer wanting to improve their putting
**Includes:** Putting stroke fundamentals; green-reading and distance control work; putter fit conversation and next-step drills

### Wedge Guru
**Description:** For golfers who want to become more efficient scorers around the green. Focuses on short-game efficiency and scoring philosophy inside key yardages.
**Who it's for:** Golfers wanting to sharpen their short game and scoring
**Includes:** Short-game efficiency review; practice plan built from observed weaknesses; scoring-focused work inside key yardages

**Pricing for Training Programs:** [Not published — direct customers to call 352-446-4456]

## Service 3: Specialty Clinics (6 Topics)

Group instruction sessions — typically 4–6 students max. One hour per topic.

**Pricing:**
- Standard rate: $30 per session
- Current Oxford Golf Academy students: $20 per session

### Putting
**Description:** Distance control and green reading. Covers stroke fundamentals, pace, and reading breaks.
**Led by:** Rick Young (Putting Specialist)

### Driver & Distance
**Description:** Tee shot confidence — keeping the ball in play and setting up easier second shots.

### Chipping & Pitching
**Description:** Short-game touch and recovery. Shots around the green and in the scoring zone.

### Sand & Bunker Play
**Description:** Getting out efficiently and closer to the hole. Greenside bunker basics.

### Wedge Play
**Description:** Scoring zone control and consistency with gap, sand, and lob wedges.

### Fundamentals (Clinic)
**Description:** For new players and returning golfers rebuilding basics. Covers grip, stance, posture, and ball position.

**Scheduling:** Clinic topics and availability vary seasonally. Contact academy at 352-446-4456 or visit booking page to inquire about upcoming sessions.
