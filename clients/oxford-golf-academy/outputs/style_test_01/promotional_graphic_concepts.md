# Oxford Golf Academy — Promotional Graphic Concepts (Draft)

**Status:** Draft concept file for human review only. No final images.

## Concept 1: Premium Sports-Promo Style

- **Concept name:** Premium Sports-Promo Lesson Push
- **Visual style:** Dark, premium, high-contrast sports promo with warm gold/amber accents; modern sans-serif typography; energetic but not hype-heavy.
- **Headline:** Start Strong. Build Better Golf.
- **Supporting text:** Beginner golf lessons in Oxford, Florida designed to help you feel more confident from the first swing.
- **CTA:** Book Your Lesson
- **Layout description:** Large centered headline over a dark gradient background, with one supporting line below and a bold CTA button near the bottom. Leave a clean open zone for logo placement and a subtle Oxford / The Villages local cue.
- **Visual elements:** Abstract swing arc, golf ball motion trail, subtle Toptracer-style linework, gold accent glow, simple golf iconography, understated Oxford / The Villages location cue.
- **Assets needed:** Logo file, brand-approved fonts, optional real facility photo only if client provides one.
- **Risks / client confirmations needed:**
  - Confirm use of the tagline or whether this headline direction is preferred.
  - No fake facility imagery.
  - No claims about guaranteed improvement or results.
  - No prices, offers, or testimonials.

## Concept 2: Clean Beginner-Friendly Lesson Ad

- **Concept name:** Welcome to the Game
- **Visual style:** Bright, clean, accessible lesson ad with open space, simple structure, and a welcoming tone.
- **Headline:** New to Golf? Start Here.
- **Supporting text:** Beginner-friendly instruction at Oxford Golf Academy in Oxford, Florida.
- **CTA:** Explore Programs
- **Layout description:** Simple top-to-bottom layout with headline first, short supporting copy under it, and a clear CTA button. Use one friendly focal graphic element and keep the design uncluttered.
- **Visual elements:** Minimal golf ball/tee graphic, soft geometric shapes, subtle course-inspired lines, optional silhouette-free golf motif.
- **Assets needed:** Logo file, brand colors, optional instructor name if client wants a named instructor featured.
- **Risks / client confirmations needed:**
  - Confirm whether “New to Golf?” is the right audience hook.
  - Do not imply walk-ins, schedule availability, or special pricing unless confirmed.
  - Avoid resort/country-club visuals.

## Concept 3: Educational “Practice With a Plan” Style

- **Concept name:** Practice With Purpose
- **Visual style:** Educational, modern, and data-informed; uses a clean grid/card layout with instructional energy rather than promo energy.
- **Headline:** Practice With a Plan.
- **Supporting text:** Build confidence with focused beginner lessons and clear next steps.
- **CTA:** Call for Help Choosing
- **Layout description:** Split layout with headline and short copy on one side, a simple three-step or icon-based learning sequence on the other. Leave space for logo and avoid crowding, with a subtle Oxford / The Villages local cue.
- **Visual elements:** Three-step visual flow, target icon, swing path line, small data-style accents inspired by Toptracer, clean typography blocks, understated Oxford / The Villages location cue.
- **Assets needed:** Logo file, brand colors, optional real Toptracer/facility photo if available.
- **Risks / client confirmations needed:**
  - Keep wording general; do not claim measurable improvement.
  - If Toptracer is referenced visually, keep it accurate and non-overpromising.
  - No fake stats, no invented lesson outcomes.
