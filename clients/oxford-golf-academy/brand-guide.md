## Visual Style

**Photography style:** Natural, on-location. Real people at the range or on the course. Not stock-photo-generic.
**Preferred image subjects:** The actual range, Toptracer screens, golfers practicing, instructor with student, the facility itself
**Avoid:** Generic golf stock photos that could be from any course. Fake action shots. Anything that looks like it came from a template.

## Tone of Voice

**Adjectives that describe the brand voice:**
Local, friendly, professional, clear, helpful, approachable, genuine

**Example of the brand sounding right:**
> "Whether you've never picked up a club or just want to get more consistent, we can help. Come take a swing at Oxford Golf Academy."

**Example of the brand sounding wrong:**
> "Oxford Golf Academy is your premier destination for world-class golf instruction and cutting-edge practice technology."

## Platform-Specific Notes

**Facebook:** Conversational. First names OK if owner approves. Clear CTA every post. Emojis OK in moderation.
**Instagram:** Shorter caption, 3-5 relevant hashtags, visual-first.
**Google Business Profile:** Professional tone, no hashtags, include city/location naturally, specific service focus.
**Website copy:** Clear, trust-building, locally grounded. No superlatives without evidence.

## What This Brand Is NOT

- Not a high-end private club
- Not a corporate chain
- Not a hype-heavy fitness/sports brand
- Not generic — every piece of content should be specific to Oxford Golf Academy and the local area
