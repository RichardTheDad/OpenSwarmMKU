## Typography

> Exact font families could not be extracted from the redesign via automated fetch.
> The visual feel and structure are described below based on observed content hierarchy.
> **Confirm font names with the designer.**

**Observed typographic character from the redesign:**
- Headings: Strong, confident, modern — consistent with a premium sports instruction brand
- Body text: Clean, readable, practical — no decorative or serif-heavy feel
- CTA buttons: Clear, action-forward labels ("Book Your Lesson", "Explore Programs", "Book online now")
- Overall feel: Modern sans-serif — not traditional or script-based

**Hierarchy observed:**
- Large headline: tagline-level ("Elevate Your Game. Experience Excellence.")
- Section headings: Descriptive but punchy (service names, pillar names)
- Body copy: Short paragraphs, practical descriptions, no long blocks of text
- CTAs: Short, verb-led action phrases

**Action required:** Provide primary heading font and body font names/weights.

## Photography and Imagery Style (from redesign)

**Use:**
- Real photography of the actual facility, range, and Toptracer bays
- Action shots: instructors with students, golfers practicing, real range sessions
- Hero image: Toptracer range bays at sunset — warm, aspirational, real
- Team photography: Professional staff portraits

**Do not use:**
- Generic golf stock photos (golfer silhouettes, landscape courses, posed handshakes)
- Imagery that does not represent the Oxford Golf Academy facility
- Overly staged or artificially produced imagery
- Old website's photography style unless it matches the redesign's aesthetic
- AI-generated scenes presented as the real facility
- Golf course landscapes, clubhouse imagery, or aerial views not confirmed as the actual location
- Imagery that implies a luxury resort, private country club, or upscale golf property

**When real facility photos are not available:**
Do not substitute AI-generated or stock facility imagery. Use brand-forward graphic design instead — see **Visual Asset Restrictions** section below.

**Image style:** Modern, practical, and warm — real people, real instruction, real facility.

## Graphic Style (from redesign)

**Overall design direction:**
- Modern and premium, not traditional or country-club-stuffy
- Accessible and community-rooted, not exclusive or elitist
- Clean, spacious, professional
- Action-oriented: the graphics support a "you can improve here" message

**Premium vs. casual feel:** Premium-leaning but accessible. Not a resort brand. Not a budget brand. Local expert.

## Tone of Voice (from redesign)

See `approved-language.md` for full language guidance.

**In one line:** Confident, local, practical, and welcoming — the golf pro next door who knows what they're doing.

**What to sound like:**
- Results-focused without sounding like a sales pitch
- Expert without being condescending to beginners
- Local without being provincial — Oxford, FL matters
- Modern and clean without being corporate

**What NOT to sound like:**
- Generic AI filler ("In today's fast-paced world...")
- Corporate buzzwords ("leverage," "synergize," "world-class solutions")
- Hype-heavy sports marketing ("UNLEASH YOUR POTENTIAL!")
- Outdated country-club stiffness ("Welcome to the distinguished...")

- premium clubhouse visuals
- polished resort-like range photos

- fake high-end golf property imagery

- clean graphic shapes

- dark premium branded backgrounds
- typography-first layouts
- minimal icon-based sports visuals

## Preferred Promo Graphic Style (Canonical)

This is the approved visual direction for Oxford Golf Academy promotional graphics. All AI-generated promo graphics must follow this spec unless the client explicitly approves a different direction.

**Format:** 1080×1080 px square PNG (Facebook/Instagram default)

**Background:** Deep charcoal or near-black with warm gold/amber radial glow and subtle grain texture. No flat solid black. No real-world scenes. No facility, sky, grass, or course imagery.

**Typography:**
- Bold white or gold headline — large, centered, visually dominant
- Clean modern sans-serif only
- 1–2 line subhead in white or light gray
- Short verb-led CTA ("Book Your Lesson", "Call 352-446-4456")

**CTA button:** Gold/amber filled, rounded corners, dark contrasting text. Lower-center placement.

**Golf motifs (optional):** Abstract swing arc lines, golf ball with motion blur or glow, club silhouette, tee icon, Toptracer-style trajectory lines. All in gold/amber on dark. No people unless explicitly requested and clearly abstract.

**Logo:** Applied via Pillow overlay after generation. Clean open zone reserved in upper-left. Never drawn by AI.

**Internal labels:** Never on the image. "Concept Draft", "Draft", "Needs client asset", and all workflow notes belong in the companion markdown only.

**This style is NOT:**
- Luxury resort or country club aesthetic
- Stock-photo sports composite
- Scene-based composition with real or implied location
- Generic golf brand template
