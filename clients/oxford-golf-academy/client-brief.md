## Target Customer

**Primary audience:**
- Recreational golfers in Oxford, FL and The Villages area who want to improve
- Beginners who've never played or just started — feel welcomed, not judged
- Retirees in The Villages with time to play and practice
- Golfers wanting purposeful, data-backed practice (Toptracer appeal)
- Families and groups looking for a social experience at the range

**What they want:**
- Real improvement that shows on the course, not just at the range
- Instruction from credentialed, experienced coaches
- A local facility that feels like it knows them — not a chain or resort
- Value: getting results without spending a fortune

**What the business solves:**
- Local access to real, credentialed golf instruction (no driving to a distant course)
- Structured improvement paths (programs, clinics) not just random range sessions
- Affordable group clinic option ($30/$20) for golfers who can't afford frequent private lessons
- Club repair that extends equipment life without buying new
